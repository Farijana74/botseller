Terms and contact

This page explains the terms for using the store, service delivery and how to reach support.

1. Account and sign-in

  • Email sign-in uses a one-time code. The code is valid for 10 minutes and becomes invalid after several failed attempts.
  • A sign-in session in your browser lasts up to 30 days. Logging out ends the session in that browser.
  • You are responsible for keeping your email access and sign-in codes private.

2. Purchase and payment

  • Prices are shown in toman at the time you place the order.
  • Some payments are confirmed after a receipt is reviewed manually. Do not pay again until the order status is clear.
  • If you paid but did not receive the service, keep your order ID and follow up through a support ticket.

3. Service delivery and subscription links

  • Each subscription link is confidential and should not be shared publicly.
  • After an order is approved, the link appears on the order result page or in your account services.

4. Linking an email account to Telegram

Linking happens only after you explicitly confirm it in the Telegram bot, and only for a Telegram account that has no existing orders, services or tickets. The confirmation message shows the email being linked. If you did not request it, do not confirm.

5. Refunds

Refund conditions and any exceptions are stated on this page. For a specific case, contact support through a ticket.

6. Acceptable use

The service may not be used for illegal activity, attacks on servers or disruption of others. Violations may lead to suspension or cancellation of the service.

7. Contact support

  • Use the Tickets section in your store account, or the store's Telegram bot.
  • When following up on an order, include your order ID in your message.
